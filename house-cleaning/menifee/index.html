Two Menifees, two kinds of cleaning

Menifee is the most split city we serve, and it is worth being direct about it. New builds and construction dust

Menifee is still under construction in places, and that has a practical consequence people underestimate: fine construction dust travels a long way, gets into vents, window tracks and blinds, and keeps arriving for as long as the tract behind you is being finished.

If you have just taken delivery of a new build, a deep clean before you move furniture in is worth more than the same clean three months later — the tracks, sills and vents are the parts that matter and they are only easy to reach while the house is empty. If you are living next to active construction, expect to want deep cleans more often than the once-or-twice-a-year we suggest generally.

Cleaning for Sun City and 55+ homes

A large share of our Menifee work is in Sun City and the surrounding 55+ communities, and it is some of the work we are most careful about. These homes tend to be smaller and already well-kept, so the cleaning itself is straightforward — what matters is everything around it.

We send the same team every visit, and we keep arrival times tight rather than giving a vague window, because a morning spent waiting is a real cost to someone who arranged their day around it. If you are booking on behalf of a parent, tell us at booking and we will make sure you get the confirmations too.

Moving in or out of Menifee

With this much new construction and turnover, moves are constant here. Our move out clean includes inside the oven, refrigerator and cabinets as standard rather than as add-ons — the items landlords and property managers check first. Leaving a rental, book after the movers and keep utilities on until after we have been; those two things cause most rescheduled jobs.
