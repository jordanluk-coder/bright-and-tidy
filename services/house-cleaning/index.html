The clean that keeps a home running

Most people who hire a cleaner are not looking for a spectacular one-off. They are looking to stop losing Saturday mornings to the same rotation of kitchen, bathrooms, floors — and to have it done properly and predictably by someone they trust in their home.

That is what our standard clean, The Refresh, is for. It covers the whole home on a schedule you set, and because you keep the same team, the work gets better over time rather than restarting from zero each visit. They learn that the guest bath is barely used, that the dog bed needs doing weekly, that you would rather they skip the office.

Choosing a schedule

The right frequency depends on how hard your home works, not on how large it is.

  • Weekly — homes with young children, multiple pets, or anyone working from home full time. Nothing ever gets far enough ahead of you to become a project. Saves 20%.
  • Bi-weekly — the most common choice by a wide margin, and the one we suggest if you are unsure. Two weeks is roughly how long a home stays genuinely comfortable before the bathrooms and floors start asking again. Saves 15%.
  • Monthly — for smaller households, or people who keep on top of surface tidying themselves but want the real work handled properly. Saves 10%.
  • One-time — before guests, after a party, or as a trial run before committing to a schedule. No commitment either way.

Our memberships are no-contract. The price is locked for 12 months, but you can pause or cancel any time with 14 days' notice — the 12 months is a price guarantee, not a commitment you are tied into.

Should the first clean be a deep clean?

Often, yes — and we will tell you honestly rather than upselling. If a home has not been professionally cleaned in six months or more, there is accumulated work (baseboards, grout, inside appliances, vents) that a standard visit is not scoped to reach. Starting with a deep clean and then moving to recurring standard visits is usually cheaper over a year than paying for repeated standard cleans that never quite catch up. If your home is already in good shape, skip it.

From $183 for a home under 1,000 sq ft as a one-time visit, priced by square footage from there. Recurring plans save 10% (monthly), 15% (bi-weekly) or 20% (weekly). Flat rates, confirmed before the visit.

How often should I have my house cleaned?

Bi-weekly suits most households and is our most common schedule. Weekly suits homes with kids, pets or heavy traffic. Monthly works if you tidy between visits but want the deeper work handled.

No — most clients aren't. Many leave a key, garage code or smart-lock code, stored securely and shared only with your assigned team.
