Why turnovers are a different job

A residential clean has a flexible deadline. A turnover has a hard one, and the consequence of missing it is a guest standing in a driveway. The work itself is also different: the property has to look identical to its listing photos, not merely clean — pillows in the right arrangement, remotes where the guest expects them, the coffee station reset.

Temecula Valley makes this sharper than most markets. Weekend wine-country demand means Friday and Sunday back-to-backs are the norm rather than the exception, and a property that turns over cleanly on a Sunday afternoon in October is doing so because it was scheduled in advance, not because someone was free.

What a turnover includes

  • Beds stripped, remade with fresh linens
  • Towels swapped, folded and staged
  • Kitchen reset, dishes and appliances
  • Refrigerator cleared of guest leftovers
  • Bathrooms sanitized and restaged
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Trash and recycling out, liners replaced
  • Consumables restocked from your supply
  • Surfaces, switches and handles disinfected
  • Furniture and decor returned to listing layout
  • Patio or balcony tidied and swept
  • Damage and low-stock report with photos

How we work with hosts

We schedule around your calendar, not ours

Give us your standard checkout and check-in times when you set the property up and we book inside that window. For hosts with steady occupancy, we hold recurring slots rather than treating each turnover as a fresh booking — which is what makes same-day back-to-backs reliable instead of hopeful.

The same team, every time

Short-term rentals depend on consistency, and consistency is a staffing question. Your property gets the same cleaners, so they know that the bedding is stored in the hall closet, that the patio gate sticks, and what the living room is supposed to look like — without a checklist being re-read each visit.

You hear about problems before your guest does

If we find damage, a stain, a missing item or a supply about to run out, you get photos and a short message the same day. Airbnb and Vrbo damage claims are time-sensitive and evidence-based, so a time-stamped photo taken between guests is worth considerably more than a report after the next stay has muddied whose damage it was.

Linens and supplies

We strip and remake beds and swap towels from the spare sets you keep on site. Hosts running back-to-backs generally keep two or three sets per bed, so the turnover never waits on a wash cycle — if you are running one set, that is the first thing worth changing. We can run laundry on site as an add-on when the window allows, and we restock the consumables you stock, but we do not shop for supplies on your behalf.

Mid-stay and deep cleans

Two other things hosts book regularly. Mid-stay cleans on long bookings, which many guests on week-plus stays expect and which keep a property from absorbing two weeks of wear at once. And a seasonal deep clean — turnovers keep a property presentable, but they are not the moment to be degreasing an oven or descaling a shower. Most hosts we work with schedule a deep clean quarterly, in a gap between bookings.

What it costs

Turnovers are priced by property size, on the same square-footage basis as the rest of our work, and hosts with regular bookings are on a recurring rate rather than one-time pricing. Because turnover scope varies more than residential cleaning — linen handling, laundry, how much restaging a property needs — we set a per-turnover rate with you rather than quoting blind.

Frequently asked questions

Can you turn over between an 11am checkout and a 4pm check-in?

Yes — that's the standard window we work in. Tell us your checkout and check-in times when you set the property up and we schedule inside them. Same-day back-to-backs are normal here on weekends, so the earlier we have your calendar, the better.

Do you handle linens and towels?

Yes, provided you keep spare sets on site. Most hosts running back-to-backs keep two or three sets per bed so the turnover never waits on a wash cycle. We can run laundry on site as an add-on when the schedule allows.

Will you tell me if a guest damaged something?

Yes — photos of any damage, stains or missing items, sent before the next guest arrives, so you have time-stamped evidence while a claim is still possible.

Do you restock supplies?

We restock the consumables you leave on site — toilet paper, paper towels, soap, coffee, dish tabs — and flag anything running low. We don't purchase supplies on your behalf.

What if a guest checks out late?

Tell us as soon as you know. We'll shift the crew's route where we can. If a late checkout eats the whole window, we'd rather call you and agree a plan than arrive and rush a job that has to look perfect.

Do you work with Vrbo and direct bookings too?

Yes — the platform makes no difference to us. Anything run as a short-term rental works the same way.
