What a move out clean actually has to accomplish

A move out clean is not a bigger version of a regular clean. A regular clean is judged by how a home feels to live in. A move out clean is judged by one person walking through an empty unit with a checklist, looking specifically for the things tenants miss — and every item they find comes out of your deposit.

That is why our move out service, The Fresh Start, is organized around inspection points rather than rooms. The inside of the oven, the inside of the refrigerator, and the inside of cabinets and drawers are included as standard, because those three are the most common deductions on a move-out statement. On a standard or deep clean, those same items are paid add-ons.

What's included

Everything in our deep clean, plus the empty-home detail work:

  • Inside the oven, degreased
  • Inside the refrigerator and freezer
  • Inside all cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Cabinet and drawer fronts, handles
  • All baseboards, hand-wiped
  • Door frames, doors and switch plates
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Light fixtures and ceiling fans
  • Bathrooms: grout, fixtures, exhaust vents
  • Showers and tubs descaled
  • Sinks, faucets and mirrors polished
  • Appliance exteriors and behind where reachable
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped and edged
  • Cobwebs and corner dust removed

Interior windows, laundry and dishes are available as add-ons if the unit needs them. Carpet shampooing and exterior window washing are not services we offer — if a lease requires professional carpet cleaning, we will tell you so you can book a carpet specialist alongside us rather than discovering the gap at the walkthrough.

Apartment move out cleaning

Apartments and condos make up a large share of this work, and they are inspected more rigidly than houses — property management companies tend to use a standardized form, so the same items come up every time.

Apartments are also faster and cheaper to clean than houses, simply because there is less square footage. A one-bedroom unit is at the bottom of our pricing, and our estimator prices by size rather than by bedroom count, so a compact two-bedroom is not charged like a large one.

A few things worth knowing if you are leaving an apartment:

  • Book the clean after the movers, not before. A unit cleaned while boxes are still stacked will have to be re-cleaned in the areas they covered.
  • Leave utilities on. We need hot water and power. Tenants who schedule their shut-off for the morning of the clean are the single most common cause of a rescheduled job.
  • Check whether your lease specifies carpet cleaning. Many Inland Empire apartment leases require a professional carpet receipt, which is separate from what we do.
  • Patch nail holes first if you plan to. Spackle dust travels, so patching after a clean undoes part of it.

Move in cleaning for a new apartment

The other direction matters too. A unit that has been sitting vacant, or that was cleaned by whoever the property manager could get cheapest, is worth cleaning before your things go in — it is the only time you will ever have clear access to the inside of every cabinet and closet. Same service, same price, and it is far quicker when the unit is empty.

Rental and lease-end cleaning

For rental houses, the stakes are usually larger deposits and a private landlord rather than a management company, which cuts both ways: the inspection may be less formal, but disagreements are less predictable.

We work with both tenants and the people on the other side of the lease — landlords and small property owners across the valley book us between tenancies, and Realtors book us before listing photos. If you are the owner rather than the tenant, tell us at booking; turnovers between tenants often need the same detail work but on a tighter window.

Two practical notes for renters:

  • Keep the receipt. A dated, itemized invoice from an insured cleaning company is useful documentation if a deposit is disputed. Ours is emailed automatically after the clean.
  • Book before the walkthrough, not the same day. Leaving a day of buffer means that if something needs attention, our 24-hour guarantee can still fix it before anyone inspects.

Frequently asked questions

How much does move out cleaning cost?

The Fresh Start begins at $345 for a home under 1,000 sq ft and is priced by square footage from there. The price is flat and confirmed before we start — no hourly meter.

Does it include inside the oven and refrigerator?

Yes — inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, and inside cabinets and closets are all included rather than charged as add-ons. Those three are what landlords check most.

Does the home need to be empty?

Ideally, yes. An empty home lets us clean inside cabinets, behind appliances and along baseboards properly. We can clean around remaining furniture, but we can't guarantee what's underneath it — and that's usually where deposit disputes start.

Do you clean apartments as well as houses?

Yes. Apartments and condos are a large share of our move out work across Temecula, Murrieta and Menifee. Smaller units are quicker and priced lower, and we follow the same inspection-focused checklist.

How far in advance should I book?

A week's notice is comfortable, and end-of-month dates fill first because most leases end then. If you're up against a walkthrough in the next day or two, call or text (951) 593-8266 and we'll tell you honestly whether we can fit you in.

Can you guarantee I get my deposit back?

No cleaning company honestly can — deposits also cover paint, carpet wear, repairs and unpaid rent, none of which are cleaning. What we guarantee is the cleaning itself: if something we were responsible for isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and redo it free.
